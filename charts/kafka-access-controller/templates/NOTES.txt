⚠ NO ANNOTATIONS ON THE SERVICE ACCOUNT. The default admin auth is MSK IAM,
  signed with whatever AWS credentials the pod has. On EKS that comes from a
  Pod Identity Association or an IRSA role annotation on
  {{ include "kafka-access-controller.serviceAccountName" . }}, and the role needs

Create access for a service:

  apiVersion: kafka-access-controller.io/v1alpha1
  kind: KafkaAccess
  metadata:
    name: my-service
    namespace: {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  spec:
    cluster:
      bootstrapServers: [<broker>:9098]
      auth:
        region: <region>
    principal: User:my-service
    topics:
      - name: my-service.events
        manage: true
        partitions: 6
        operations: [Read, Write]
    consumerGroups:
      - name: my-service
        operations: [Read]

Then watch it settle:

  kubectl get kafkaaccess -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -w

Status carries what was applied; status.pending lists what the cluster still
lacks, and status.warnings the changes Kafka cannot make (fewer partitions, a
new replication factor).
